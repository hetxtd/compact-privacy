# Compact — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 22 October 2026**

Compact is an iPhone makeup mirror application developed by **Hakam Solutions Ltd**.

We believe your reflection is personal. Compact is designed to provide an illuminated mirror and beauty tools without requiring an account.

## Camera and facial guidance

Compact uses your device's front-facing camera to provide the live mirror, magnification, temporary Freeze, picture-in-picture and optional beauty guidance.

Camera frames and estimated facial landmarks are processed locally on your device. Compact does not upload your mirror images to our servers or automatically save them to your Photos library.

Compact keeps recent camera images and estimated facial landmarks temporarily in memory for the mirror, guidance, picture-in-picture and Freeze. Freeze ends when you return to Live or leave the active mirror. Processing buffers and framework caches may remain temporarily in memory until replaced or released.

Compact does not save mirror frames or facial landmarks to its own persistent storage, automatically save them to Photos, or upload them to Hakam Solutions Ltd.

## Microphone and voice commands

If you enable Hands-Free voice features, Compact requests microphone and speech-recognition permissions.

Compact configures speech recognition to require on-device processing. If on-device recognition is unavailable or the required permissions are denied, Hands-Free voice cannot start; manual controls remain available.

Compact does not enable a cloud-recognition fallback. Microphone audio is processed temporarily for commands, and recognised phrases may appear in the Hands-Free panel until Voice stops.

Compact does not create saved voice recordings or upload microphone audio to Hakam Solutions Ltd.

You can disable microphone or speech-recognition access through iOS Settings.

## Information stored on your device

Compact stores certain preferences locally, including onboarding completion, opening-sound settings and supported guidance preferences.

These settings help the app remember your choices. Some other adjustments apply only during the current session.

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting Compact from your device.

## Accounts, advertising and tracking

Compact does not require you to create an account.

The current application does not contain third-party advertising or behavioural tracking SDKs.

Compact does not use your camera images, facial landmarks or microphone audio for advertising or behavioural profiling.

## Support enquiries

If you contact Hakam Solutions Ltd, we may process your email address and the information you provide to respond to your enquiry.

We use this information for legitimate business purposes, including responding to support and privacy requests, and to comply with applicable legal obligations.

We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry, handle related support or privacy requests, and meet applicable legal obligations.

We review retained correspondence and delete or anonymise it when it is no longer needed.

Contact **nasir@hakamsolutions.com** with questions about retention or requests concerning correspondence you have provided.

## Permissions and your choices

You control Compact's camera, microphone and speech-recognition permissions through iOS Settings.

You may withdraw permissions at any time, although certain features will then become unavailable.

Where UK data protection law applies, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your personal information, object to certain processing, and make a complaint to the Information Commissioner's Office.

To exercise applicable rights concerning information you have provided directly to Hakam Solutions Ltd, contact **nasir@hakamsolutions.com**.

# Compact — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 21 October 2026**

Compact is an iPhone makeup mirror application developed by **Hakam Solutions Ltd**.

We believe your reflection is personal. Compact is designed to provide an illuminated mirror and beauty tools without requiring an account.

## Camera and facial guidance

Compact uses your device's front-facing camera to provide the live mirror, magnification, temporary Freeze and optional beauty guidance.

Camera frames and facial landmarks are processed locally on your device. Compact does not upload your mirror images to our servers or automatically save them to your Photos library.

Temporary image information is used while the relevant feature is active.

## Microphone and voice commands

If you enable Hands-Free voice features, Compact requests microphone and speech-recognition permissions.

Speech recognition is configured for on-device processing where supported. Compact does not independently record, store or upload your voice recordings to our servers. Apple's speech-recognition services and device capabilities may affect how speech is processed.

You can disable microphone or speech-recognition access in iOS Settings.

## Information stored on your device

Compact stores certain preferences locally, such as onboarding completion, opening-sound settings and supported guidance preferences.

These settings help the app remember your choices. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting Compact from your device.

## Accounts, advertising and tracking

Compact does not require you to create an account.

The current application does not contain third-party advertising or behavioural tracking SDKs.

## Support enquiries

If you contact Hakam Solutions Ltd, we may process your email address and the information you provide to answer your enquiry.

We use this information for legitimate business purposes, including responding to support and privacy requests. We retain support correspondence only for the period needed under our support-retention policy.

## Permissions and your choices

You control Compact's camera, microphone and speech-recognition permissions through iOS Settings.

You may withdraw permissions at any time, although certain features will then become unavailable.

If you have questions about personal information you have provided directly to us, you may contact us using the address below. UK data protection rights apply where relevant.

## Third-party services

Compact uses Apple's iOS frameworks and distribution services. Apple may separately process device diagnostics and App Store information according to its own privacy practices.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Compact's features or data practices change. The current version will remain available at this page.
